# Kitti – Privacy Policy

*Effective 17 November 2026*

Kitti is a card game for Android made by Sanjeeb Boyaju ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to **sanjeebboyaju@gmail.com**.

## What the game stores about you

**Your game profile.** The display name and character you pick, your chip balance, your game statistics (games played, completed and won) and your settings (sound, card deck, table options). The profile is saved on your device. It is also saved in Unity Cloud Save, so that your progress comes back if you reinstall the game.

**An anonymous player ID.** When the game starts it signs you in with Unity Authentication as an anonymous player. You do not create an account, and we do not ask for your email address or your real name. The player ID connects your device to your cloud profile and is shown on the Profile screen.

We do not collect your contacts, your photos, or your precise location.

## Online play

When you play online, the game uses Unity Multiplayer Services (Sessions, Relay and Lobby) to connect you with other players. The other players at your table see your display name, your character and your chip count. To connect devices, these services process your IP address. Kitti has no chat, voice or image sharing.

## Game settings from the server

The game downloads settings, such as how often ads may appear, from Unity Remote Config. This request includes your anonymous player ID and basic information about the app and your device.

## Advertising

Kitti shows ads through Google AdMob: short videos that you choose to watch for a reward, and occasional full-screen ads between games. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address, information about your device and how you interact with ads. It uses this to show ads, to measure them, and to prevent fraud.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before AdMob uses your information for personalised ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's settings (on Android: Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Service providers

- **Unity Technologies:** sign-in, cloud saves, online play and remote settings. See the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).
- **Google:** AdMob advertising, and Google Play for distributing the app. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We do not sell your information. We share it only with these providers, so that they can run the features described above.

## How long information is kept, and deleting it

Your profile stays on your device until you uninstall the game. The cloud copy is kept while you play. To delete your cloud profile, email **sanjeebboyaju@gmail.com** with the player ID from the Profile screen; we will delete it within 30 days. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own policy.

## Children

Kitti is meant for players aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the related data.

## Security

Information sent between the game and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
